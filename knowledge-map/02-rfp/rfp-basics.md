# RFP Basics

## Key Terms
- **PQ** — Pre-Qualification
- **TQ** — Technical Qualification
- **Compliance** — requirement adherence section
- **Corrigendum** — also called "request to change"; used to change/update terms or definitions in an already-published RFP
- **QCBS** — Quality Cost Based Selection (a type of tender/evaluation method)

## Important Points When Creating an RFP
1. **Check client's requirement first** — every point included must be project-relevant.
   - Example: A provider's feature like "strong outdoor durability" is irrelevant if the project is purely indoor. Don't include it.
2. **If high competition exists** — additional/differentiating features can be added to strengthen the bid.

## Bidding Types

### Reactive Bidding
Suggesting/recommending something to benefit your own position within an RFP/bid that another party has already settled (i.e., not written from scratch by you) — you respond to terms someone else has already defined.

### Proactive Bidding
Your own RFP — written and bid from scratch, by your own organization. You define the requirements and shape the terms rather than responding to someone else's.

## Procurement Portals
- **GeM** — Government e-Marketplace; a government procurement portal used for RFPs/tenders from Indian government bodies and PSUs.
