# Ratings & Standards — Reference

## IP Rating (Ingress Protection)
**Standard:** IEC 60529
Format: **IP** + [dust digit] + [water digit]

| Rating | Dust Protection | Water Protection | Typically Used Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| IP66 | Dust-tight | Protected against powerful water jets | Most common outdoor camera rating |
| IP67 | Dust-tight | Protected against temporary immersion | Higher water exposure areas |

## PoE (Power over Ethernet)
- **Cat5 / Cat6e** — Cat6e can carry both video data and electrical power over the same cable
- **IEEE 802.3af (PoE)** — standard for powering fixed IP cameras *(note: raw source figure was "1.95W" — standard 802.3af delivers up to 15.4W at source / ~12.95W usable at device; verify the exact figure intended before relying on it)*
- **Cat6e max cable run:** 100 m
  - If the run exceeds 100 m: use a **12 VDC power supply + PoE injector**

## CCTV Security Standard (India)
- **STQC:ER:01** — CCTV security standard under "Make in India" rules
