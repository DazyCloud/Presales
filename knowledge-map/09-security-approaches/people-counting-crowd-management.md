# People Counting / Crowd Management — Approaches

## Two Categories

### 1. Crowd Management (not precise)
- **Crowd estimation** (prediction-based)
- Used in: **outdoor areas**

### 2. People Counting (precise)
- Used in: **controlled / indoor areas**

## Ways to Do Precise People Counting
1. **ToF (Time of Flight) sensor** — used in controlled areas
2. **3D stereo vision** — used at entries
3. **Lidar** — used in open areas
   - e.g. **Hesai** — small size, cheap (model reference: OT128)
4. **AI (camera-based)** — depends on camera quality; needs additional compute
